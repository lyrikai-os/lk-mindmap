Baseline: [mindmap-v1.1/guides/user-guide-delta.md](../../mindmap-v1.1/guides/user-guide-delta.md) and [mindmap-v1/guides/user-guide.md](../../mindmap-v1/guides/user-guide.md).

## New / clarified

| Topic | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| Undo / Redo | Use **Undo** and **Redo** in the bottom board-look bar, or ⌘Z · ⇧⌘Z (Ctrl on Windows/Linux); on desktop, the app **Edit** menu also undoes/redoes. There is no separate floating Undo chip. |
| Toolsets | Clean Studio, Sketchbook, and Pinboard sit on the **right** of the canvas. |
| Rename | Library **pencil**, topbar **title**, or overflow **⋯ → Rename** — not right-click. |
| Footer | One bar: Undo/Redo, Theme…, colors, grid, Gap, zoom, Fit. |

## Unchanged walls (disclose)

No account. Boards live in your boards folder. No collaboration. No automatic layout. Package may be unsigned.
