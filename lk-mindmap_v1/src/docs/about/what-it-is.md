# What LYRIKAI board is

LYRIKAI board is a calm place to think on your Mac. One canvas holds your ideas, sketches, notes, and images together — no switching apps to connect the dots.

## Works offline

Everything runs on your computer. You do not need an account, a sign-in, or an internet connection to open a board or keep working. Your boards stay on your machine until you move them yourself.

## Your boards, your rules

Each board is a personal workspace. Name it, rearrange it, save it, copy the file elsewhere — it is yours. There is no shared cloud workspace and no live collaboration in this version.

## Three ways to work on one canvas

Use **Clean Studio** for structured ideas and connections, **Sketchbook** for freehand drawing, and **Pinboard** for sticky notes, text, and images. They all share the same board surface, so you can mix approaches without losing context.
