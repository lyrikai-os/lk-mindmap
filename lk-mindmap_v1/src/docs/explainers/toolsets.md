# Toolsets

Three toolsets sit on the left of the canvas. Tap a name to expand or collapse it. Only one section is open at a time.

## Clean Studio

For structured thinking. **Add idea** places a labeled node. With a node selected, **Child** (or **Tab**) adds a connected child; **Sibling** (or **Shift + Return**) adds a peer on the same branch. Connectors stay attached when you move nodes.

## Sketchbook

For rough drawing. Choose **Freehand**, **Shape**, or **Connector**, then draw on the canvas. Style selected strokes with the canvas toolbar.

**Stroke preset** — before you draw, pick a stroke feel (weight and roughness). The preset applies to new strokes only; existing marks stay as you made them.

## Pinboard

For notes and reference. Add a **Sticky note**, standalone **Text**, or **Import image** (PNG, JPEG, or WebP). You can also drop or paste an image onto the board.

**Caption** — after placing an image, use Pinboard to add or edit a caption near it.

Switching toolsets does not erase or restyle work from another toolset.
