# Show boards folder in Finder

1. Look at the **library** sidebar on the left.
2. Scroll to the bottom and click **Boards folder** (folder icon with an arrow).
3. Finder opens your boards folder, showing every `.lkmindmap` file.

**Alternative** — click **⋯** in the top bar and choose **Show in Finder**. If a board is open, Finder highlights that board’s file; otherwise it opens the folder.
